EXPERIMENT NOTE

The test results show that a forecasting model can improve average error over the baselines, yet a 20% safety margin still leaves shortfalls. The allocation target of at most 1% shortfall intervals was not met in validation.

THE METHOD

What these numbers mean

Scope and limitations matter when a forecast becomes a capacity decision.

01 / SIGNAL

Recorded traffic

Nominal Mbps = recorded bytes × 8 ÷ (600 × 1,000,000). Traffic includes both directions; it is not physical link utilization.

02 / FORECAST

One interval ahead

A three-hour history predicts a single 10-minute target interval at the selected 10-, 30-, or 60-minute lead.

03 / EVALUATION

Chronological holdout

Training-only scaling, documented exclusions, and chronological splits protect evaluation. One institution and one seed limit generalisation.

See dataset and experiment limitations

MAPE excludes non-positive targets. The browser displays saved predictions and scores; model training and experiment regeneration are in the Python repository. All tested safety margins missed the 1% validation shortfall target.

The capacity experiment is an offline simulation. It does not provide live control, packet loss, latency, or routing measurements.

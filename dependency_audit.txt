FILE DEPENDENCIES AND EXECUTION ORDER
1. Acquisition: download_data.py needs Internet access to the v1 Zenodo
   record (https://zenodo.org/records/13382427). It verifies both archives
   by published MD5 and extracts the two sample files. Complete existing
   archives are reused after verification; incomplete .part files restart.
2. audit_sample.py, mark_segments.py and inspect_outage.py need:
   data/raw/cesnet/sample/institution_0.csv
   data/raw/cesnet/sample/times_10_minutes.csv
3. mark_exclusions.py needs data/inspection/institution_0_segments.csv.
4. build_features.py needs data/inspection/institution_0_review.csv.
5. prepare_dataset.py runs steps 3/4 and segment labeling automatically;
   its external inputs are the two raw samples. It produces features,
   all 10/30/60-minute windows, scalers, configuration and quality outputs.
6. evaluate_baselines.py needs matching windows_<horizon>min.npz/.csv
   and preparation_config.json in data/prepared/.
7. train_lstm.py needs those three prepared files plus
   scalers_<horizon>min.json. CPU or CUDA execution is selectable.
   Saved baselines are optional for standalone LSTM evaluation. If a
   baseline run_config.json exists, matching metrics.csv and predictions.csv
   are required for comparison; prepared-file hashes must agree. Run the
   baseline command before training for the documented comparison workflow.
   This comparison check currently occurs after training; stale/incomplete
   baseline folders should be regenerated before starting another run.
8. compare_institutions.py separately needs the COMPLETE institutions.tar.gz
   and the sample timestamp map. It is not a prerequisite for retraining the
   already-selected institution 0 pipeline.
